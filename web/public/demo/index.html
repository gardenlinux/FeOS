FeChat on FeRun

FeChat is a chat with an agent that runs commands. Every conversation gets a sandbox of its own from FeRun, a Firecracker microVM, and FeChat itself runs on FeRun as an ordinary app. So one tenant shows both halves of FeRun. This page follows one conversation from the chat window down to the trace.

sandboxes an environment of tenant chat

One sandbox per conversation, made by FeChat's backend through FeRun's public API. It pauses when nobody calls it and hibernates later.

prod another environment of tenant chat

FeChat's two services, web and api, deployed like any app, at chat.fecore.dev.

A conversation

A new conversation in FeChat. The field at the top right names the sandbox's image, here ghcr.io/maltej/debian-ai, a Debian with the usual tools. The first question needs a look at the machine.

Then something that leaves the machine.

FeChat's backend keeps no state. The browser holds the conversation and a signed reference to its sandbox, and sends both back with every message. So any replica of the backend can answer, and a restarted one finds the sandbox again.

The sandbox behind it

In FeRun's console, tenant chat, environment sandboxes: what a sandbox costs while nobody talks to it, and what it may reach.

What did the agent do in there? FeRun knows, because every tool call passes its MCP door.

Sleep and wake

After thirty minutes paused, FeRun hibernated the sandbox: the node wrote the snapshot to the object store and dropped it from its disk.

A minute after the first hibernation, the conversation went on.

FeChat as an app

The other environment of the tenant, prod, holds FeChat itself: web, an nginx with the single-page app, and api, the Go backend. Nothing here knows that the app is FeRun's own chat.
